자기소개 & 지원 동기 4

How would you introduce yourself?+
Thanks for having me today.
오늘 인터뷰 기회 주셔서 감사해요.
What have you worked on?+
I've mainly worked in data science and engineering.
주로 데이터 사이언스와 엔지니어링 일을 해왔어요.
Why loveholidays?+
I'd love to bring that experience to loveholidays.
그 경험을 loveholidays에서 활용하고 싶어요.
Why did you apply?+
I applied because the role really caught my eye.
이 직무가 눈에 들어와서 지원했어요.

프로젝트 & 성과 4

What have you worked on recently?+
I recently worked on a project to improve our credit scoring system.
최근 신용평가 시스템을 개선하는 프로젝트를 했어요.
How did it go?+
We got much better results than the previous model.
기존 모델보다 결과가 훨씬 좋아졌어요.
What are you most proud of?+
One project I'm particularly proud of is our credit scoring model.
특히 자랑스러운 프로젝트는 신용평가 모델이에요.
What did you learn?+
It showed me how important it is to understand the business problem first.
비즈니스 문제를 먼저 이해하는 게 얼마나 중요한지 배웠어요.

어려운 상황 & 갈등 4

How did you handle a tight deadline?+
We were on a tight deadline, so I had to prioritise.
일정이 촉박해서 우선순위를 정해야 했어요.
What did you do?+
I took the lead, and we managed to finish on time.
제가 주도했고 제시간에 마무리했어요.
Have you dealt with different opinions?+
We had different opinions on how to approach it.
접근 방식에 대한 의견이 달랐어요.
How did you resolve it?+
So we talked it through and agreed on a way forward.
그래서 이야기를 나누고 해결 방향을 정했어요.

예상 못 한 질문 8

Need a moment?+
That's a good question. Let me think for a second.
좋은 질문이네요. 잠깐 생각해볼게요.
Give an example+
Let me give you an example.
예를 들어볼게요.
State the issue+
I think the main issue is...
핵심 문제는…인 것 같아요.
First thought+
The first thing that comes to mind is...
가장 먼저 떠오르는 건…
Draw on experience+
Based on my experience, I'd...
제 경험상 저는…할 것 같아요.
Find an example+
Let me think of a good example.
적절한 사례를 생각해볼게요.
Explain your approach+
There are a few ways to do this. I'd probably start with...
방법이 몇 가지 있는데, 저는 우선…부터 할 것 같아요.
Explain your approach simply+
I'd probably approach it like this...
저는 아마 이렇게 접근할 것 같아요.

면접관에게 역질문 3

Success in the role+
What makes someone successful in this role?
이 직무에서 잘하는 사람은 어떤 사람인가요?
Current projects+
What projects is the team working on right now?
팀에서 지금 어떤 프로젝트를 진행하고 있나요?
First three months+
What would you expect me to achieve in my first three months?
입사 후 첫 3개월 동안 어떤 성과를 기대하시나요?

면접 마무리 3

Thank the interviewer+
Thanks for your time today. It was great speaking with you.
오늘 시간 내주셔서 감사해요. 이야기 나눠서 좋았어요.
Express interest+
Thanks, this has made me even more interested in the role.
감사해요. 이야기 나누고 나니 이 직무에 더 관심이 생겼어요.
Close naturally+
I look forward to hearing from you.
연락 기다리겠습니다.
